SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA · ROCKY SHORE FIELD GUIDE

Meet the shore at the right moment.

Oh Tidepool brings together tide windows, marine conditions, and a calm field guide for curious, respectful exploring.

Coming soon to iPhone · Join the public TestFlight beta

★
01

Plan around the tide

See the low-tide windows (the best times to explore), surf conditions, weather, and water temperature in one focused view.

02

Know the day at each shore

Each site opens with guidance written for that day: what is going on, the tide window it uses, and what each hazard does to a visit. An official caution can sit beside a good day, while a closure or restriction overrules it. A cited local story is labelled Unofficial.

03

Tips while you browse

A reviewed tidepool tip rides in the Explore header as you scroll. Swipe sideways for another, swipe back for the one before, or tap for the full tip and its source.

04

Look closer

Keep a pocket field guide to the creatures and living textures of the intertidal zone (the shore exposed between high and low tide).

SEE IT IN ACTION

From the list to the shore.

LEAVE THE POOL WILD

Observe softly. Step carefully. Return what you find.

Oh Tidepool is designed to support responsible visits—not to replace local rules, conditions, or good judgment.
