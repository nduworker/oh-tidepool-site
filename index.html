SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA · ROCKY SHORE FIELD GUIDE

Meet the shore at the right moment.

Oh Tidepool brings together tide windows, marine conditions, and a calm field guide for curious, respectful exploring.

Coming soon to iPhone · Join the public TestFlight beta

★
01

Plan around the tide

See the low-tide windows (the best times to explore), surf conditions, weather, and water temperature in one focused view, across 14 mapped sites. Every list card carries a ribbon naming the kind of place: Tidepool or Discovery site. Plan-visit alerts watch the tide and the weather, and name surf inside the notification.

02

Know the day at each shore

Each site opens with guidance written for that day: what is going on, the tide window it uses, and what each hazard does to a visit. Surf above a site's guide is a caution to go with care, not a block; a high tide still closes the window. An official caution can sit beside a good day, a discovery site's estimate is marked Best estimate, and a cited local story is labelled Unofficial. That line is often absent, because a story without a date is left out rather than published.

03

Tips while you browse

A reviewed tidepool tip rides in the Explore header as you scroll, drawn at random from 68 reviewed tips and moving on every five minutes. Swipe sideways for another, swipe back for the one before, or tap for the full tip and its source. It has no close button by design; Settings turns the row off.

04

Look closer

Keep a pocket field guide to the creatures and living textures of the intertidal zone (the shore exposed between high and low tide).

LEAVE THE POOL WILD

Oh Tidepool is designed to support responsible visits—not to replace local rules, conditions, or good judgment.
