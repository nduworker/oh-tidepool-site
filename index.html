In return, we ask you to

  1. Explore responsibly. Don't pick up and take home souvenirs. Tidepools are for everyone, so leave nature undisturbed.
  2. If you see trash, plastic, or bottles, carry them to the nearest trash can. They can harm the animals and plants that live there.
  3. Report misconduct, and when it is safe, try to stop people from damaging the habitat.
  4. Read the tips at the top of the app to learn how we can better protect the environment and save biodiversity for future generations.
  5. Enjoy your time at the tidepool!

  • Explore tidepool sites

    Browse a growing collection of reviewed tidepool sites, each with visit information, access notes, and the best low-tide windows coming up.

  • Know the conditions

    See tides, weather, and ocean conditions from public data sources in one place, so you can tell at a glance whether a visit looks good.

  • Get low-tide alerts

    Turn on alerts for the sites you care about and get a heads-up when a good tidepool window is coming up.

  • Learn what lives there

    Discover the sea stars, anemones, urchins, mussels, and seaweeds you're most likely to see at each site, listed most common first.

  • Stay safe

    The ocean can be dangerous. Watch the waves, never turn your back on the sea, and follow posted rules and closures. Oh Tidepool is a planning aid, not a safety guarantee: always check local conditions and official guidance before you go.

New sites and regions are added over time.
