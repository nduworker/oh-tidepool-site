Oh Tidepool support

Oh Tidepool is a free iPhone field guide and trip-planning companion for Southern California tidepools. It shows reviewed tidepool sites, NOAA tide forecasts, surf and weather, a field guide to the animals you may see, and optional low-tide alerts.

Contact

Email support@nduwork.com with questions, problems, or corrections to a site. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and the site you were looking at.

Frequently asked questions

Does the app work offline?

Yes, for what it has already downloaded. Sites, photos, the field guide, tips, and the latest daily conditions are saved on your iPhone and stay available without a connection; the app checks for new content when it is back online. Live tide, surf, and weather readings need a connection.

How do alerts work?

Alerts are optional notifications your iPhone schedules for itself. Choose sites and the times you are free in the Alerts tab, and the app lets you know about forecast windows that meet each site's tide and surf guidance. Forecasts change, so check conditions again before you go.

Why does the app ask for my location?

Location is optional. If you allow it, your iPhone uses it to show nearby tidepools; it is not sent to Oh Tidepool. The app works fully without it.

Can the app tell me a tidepool is safe or open?

No. Oh Tidepool is a planning aid. Safety, closures, and water-quality advisories are issued by the agencies that manage each shore, such as California State Parks, the National Park Service, and county health departments. Follow posted signs and lifeguards, watch the waves, and turn back when conditions do not match the plan.

How do I report a wrong photo or site detail?

Email support@nduwork.com with the site name and what looks wrong. Photo credits and licences are listed on the app image credits page.

Privacy

The app has no accounts, analytics, or advertising. See the privacy policy for what the app keeps on your device and what it downloads.
